What a first visit looks like

Sixty minutes. You'll leave with an assessment, a treatment, and a plan — not a package to sign.

0 – 15 minConsultation. What happened, what you've tried, what you need to get back to.
15 – 30 minPhysical assessment. Movement, strength, and where the pain actually originates.
30 – 50 minFirst treatment. Manual work, and machine therapy where it helps.
50 – 60 minYour plan. Rough session count, home exercises, and what to avoid this week.
